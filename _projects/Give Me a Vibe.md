# Give Me a Vibe

![](/data/images/give-me-a-vibe/result.png)

## One button for people who don't know what to build

**Press one giant button and Gemini invents something for you to vibe-code — then a deliberately anti-sycophantic verdict explains why it's a terrible product but an exceptional weekend project.**

There is no input box, no account, and no history. You pick one of four moods — Useful, Weird, Absurd, Satirical — and the model does the rest: a titled concept with its own dynamically chosen sections, metadata tags, and a verdict that refuses to call every idea amazing. Randomness comes from the model itself, a fresh seed and rotating variation lens per request, not a hardcoded list of ideas.

- **Four vibe modes** — the mode materially changes generation and tone, not just styling
- **Dynamic sections** — the model picks its own structure per idea, from audience to technical spine
- **Honest verdicts** — a good idea and a good business are judged separately
- **Build prompts** — an opt-in second call turns a surviving idea into instructions for your coding agent
