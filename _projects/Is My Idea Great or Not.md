# Is My Idea Great or Not?

![](/data/images/is-my-idea/result.png)

## An AI judge that tries to destroy your idea first

**Submit literally anything — "Should I build this SaaS?" or "What if I add raw eggs ramen-style to my pasta?" — and a deliberately anti-sycophantic AI tries to kill the idea before deciding whether anything good survives.**

The judge acts as skeptic first, prosecutor second, and fair judge only at the end. It invents its own evaluation dimensions for every idea (flavor compatibility for a recipe, willingness to pay for a SaaS pitch), decides how deep the analysis should go, and delivers the verdict as a dramatic editorial report — oversized score, punchline, prosecution, and, on rare occasions, a reluctant admission that the idea is actually good.

- **Fair / Horrible mode** — a brutality toggle that meaningfully changes the model's behavior
- **Dynamic scorecard** — 2–9 model-chosen dimensions per idea, never a fixed rubric
- **Plot Twist section** — appears only when the model finds something genuinely surprising
- **Shareable verdicts** — copy the roast card or export it as a PNG
