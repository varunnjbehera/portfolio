## 🎯 The Design Philosophy

Every theme in this pack is built on three non-negotiable technical pillars:

1. **The Asymmetric Split:** A balanced but dynamic layout that prioritizes high-impact typography on the left and a powerful portrait presence on the right.
2. **Interactive Presence:** All themes implement a sophisticated **Grayscale → Color** transition on image hover, creating a sense of "awakening" the page as the user interacts.
3. **Ultrawide Stability:** Unlike standard templates that stretch or distort on large monitors, these themes use a strict `max-w-screen-xl` safe-zone, ensuring a flawless experience from a mobile phone to a 49" ultrawide display.

## 🏆 Curator's Top 3 Picks

If you're not sure where to start, these three themes represent the peak of the collection's versatility:

1. **`architectural-swiss`** — The most technically complete theme in the pack. A clip-path portrait, a mix-blend-difference navigation that inverts against page content, a data-rich stats overlay, and a fixed footer. The only theme with a full page anatomy. Start here if you want to see what this pack is capable of.
2. **`luxury-boutique`** — The definitive "high-ticket consultant" page. Impeccable whitespace, a restrained gold accent line, and the cleanest code in the collection. It doesn't try to impress — it simply is impressive.
3. **`editorial-luxury`** — The most refined interaction in the pack. The `.btn-luxury` hover triggers a simultaneous letter-spacing shift and background color sweep — a level of CSS polish matched nowhere else in the collection. The editorial serif/sans pairing and the 3D offset image frame give it a collector's-edition feel that the other themes don't reach.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Choose Your Identity
Browse the `/signature` folder and open any `index.html` file in your browser to preview the theme.

### 2. Customize Your Content
Open the `index.html` of your chosen theme in any text editor:
- **Portrait:** Find the `<img>` tag and replace the `src` with your own portrait (Recommended aspect ratio: 4:5).
- **Copy:** Update the `<h1>` and `<p>` tags with your name, title, and value proposition.
- **Links:** Update the navigation and social media links in the `nav` and `footer` sections.

### 3. Tweak the Visuals
Every theme includes a `README.md` with a **Visual Identity Guide**, detailing the primary colors and font pairings used. You can easily swap these by changing the Tailwind classes (e.g., change `text-indigo-600` to `text-emerald-600`).
